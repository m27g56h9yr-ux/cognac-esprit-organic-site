Pineau des Charentes

Red Pineau

Red Pineau des Charentes made with Merlot and Ugni Blanc, with a supple, generous fruit expression.

Red Pineau des Charentes made with Merlot and Ugni Blanc, with a supple, generous fruit expression.

This red Pineau extends the Esprit Organic range in a more colourful register: a round, fresh and fruity palate, suited to aperitifs, fruit desserts or lightly chilled service.

Tasting

Tasting markers

Tasting

This red Pineau des Charentes is made with Merlot and Ugni Blanc. It shows a deep, bright red colour. The nose suggests ripe red fruit, cherry and a touch of plum. The palate is supple, broad and generous, with a fruity, lightly spicy finish.

Sensory notes

Sensory notes

  • Mouth:Ripe red fruit, cherry, plum, grape sweetness
  • Colour:Deep red, ruby highlights, bright
  • Nose:Fruity and generous, with cherry, blackberry and candied red fruit
  • Palate:Supple, round and fruity, with fine freshness
  • Finish:Generous, fruity and lightly spicy
