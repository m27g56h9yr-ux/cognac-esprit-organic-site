Trade resources

Product sheets and trade resources

This page brings together the key information needed to present Cognac Esprit Organic accurately: range, aromatic profiles, bottle sizes, alcohol by volume, grape varieties, awards and tasting sheets.

It helps wine merchants, importers, hospitality teams and partners prepare clear selections without adding prices, stock, customer reviews or claims not published by the house.

For United States or other international trade enquiries, use these sheets with the importer page or contact the house directly. Availability, market coverage and commercial terms are confirmed case by case.
