Organic Cognac Producer in France

Esprit Organic, organic Cognac from the Cognac region.

Esprit Organic is a family organic Cognac brand led by Léopold and Fanny Croizet in Charente, France.

The range includes VS, VSOP, Napoléon, XO, XXO, Single Cask, white Pineau and red Pineau.

From Charente, France

30 Rue d'Angoulême, 16200 Triac-Lautrait, in the Cognac region.

Organic identity

A family-led organic Cognac range for partners looking for provenance, clarity and a direct producer relationship.
