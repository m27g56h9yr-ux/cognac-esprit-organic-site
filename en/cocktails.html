01

White Pineau + Cognac VS

The Golden Hour

Freshness and organic elegance.

A fruity, vegetal cocktail with gentle Charentais melon notes, lifted by Cognac Esprit Organic. Very refreshing, low in alcohol, perfect for summer.

Ingredients

  • 70 ml White Pineau Esprit Organic
  • 20 ml Cognac VS
  • 100 g melon charentais
  • 4 cucumber slices
  • 6 fresh mint leaves
  • 1 dash of lime
  • A few ice cubes

Preparation

  1. Place all ingredients in a blender with a few ice cubes.
  2. Blend for 15 seconds.
  3. Serve immediately in a glass filled with ice. Garnish with a mint leaf.

Tasting style

  • Refreshing
  • Fresh
  • Fruity

02

Foundation VS + Ginger Beer

Cognac Mule

Naturally refreshing.

Fresh lime meets spicy ginger notes and the fruity character of Cognac Esprit Organic Foundation VS.

Ingredients

  • 4 cl Cognac Foundation VS
  • 1.5 cl fresh lime juice
  • 12 cl Ginger Beer
  • 1 lime slice
  • A few ice cubes

Preparation

  1. Fill a copper mug with ice.
  2. Pour in the Cognac Foundation VS.
  3. Add the fresh lime juice.
  4. Top up with Ginger Beer.
  5. Stir gently.
  6. Garnish with a lime slice.

Tasting style

  • Fresh
  • Tonic
  • Spicy
  • Refreshing

03

Pineaux + Cognac VSOP

Le Charente Spritz

The contemporary aperitif.

The fruity, sparkling alliance of our Pineaux and Cognac VSOP, lifted by the sweetness of Charentais melon. Fresh, light and irresistibly summery.

Ingredients

  • 30 ml White Pineau Esprit Organic
  • 30 ml Red Pineau Esprit Organic
  • 20 ml Cognac VSOP Esprit Organic
  • 80 g melon charentais
  • Sparkling water
  • A few ice cubes

Preparation

  1. Blend the Charentais melon with the white Pineau, red Pineau and Cognac VSOP.
  2. Pour into a glass filled with ice.
  3. Top up with sparkling water.
  4. Stir gently and enjoy immediately.

Tasting style

  • Fruity
  • Sparkling
  • Fresh
