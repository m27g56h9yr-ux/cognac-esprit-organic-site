Presented as the first XXO in organic agriculture, soft, structured and fruit-forward.

We are very proud to present the first XXO in organic agriculture. This Cognac is made from a blend of cognac spirits, the youngest of which is 14 years old. It is structured and very fruity. The cognac spirits in this XXO aged in new Limousin-type, wide-grain oak barrels.

Tasting

Tasting markers

Roundness, softness and delicacy

After several years of tannic extraction, they completed their ageing in older red barrels in the humid cellars of the family estate. This environment gives the cognac spirits roundness and softness. That softness allowed us to keep it at 43.5% rather than 40%, avoiding excessive dilution of its delicate aromas. Léopold CROIZET

Sensory notes
