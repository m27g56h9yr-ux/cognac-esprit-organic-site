Tasting

This white Pineau des Charentes is made from a blend of Cognac distillates and grape must from our Colombard and Ugni Blanc varieties. It has aged for many years in oak casks, giving it its bright amber colour. The palate shows candied fruit and vanilla notes. It is a rich, generous Pineau, well structured with intense and harmonious notes.

Sensory notes

Sensory notes

  • Mouth:Brioche, fresh grape juice, pear, prune, vanilla
  • Colour:Golden yellow, straw yellow, bright
  • Nose:Elaborate and balanced. Fine combination of fruity notes, fresh grape and pear, with vanilla
  • Palate:Rich, generous, complex
  • Finish:Fruity, intense, generous
