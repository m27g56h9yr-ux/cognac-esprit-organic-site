Esprit Organic, organic Cognac from the Cognac region.

Esprit Organic is a family organic Cognac brand led by Léopold and Fanny Croizet in Charente, France.

The range includes VS, VSOP, Napoléon, XO, XXO, Single Cask, white Pineau and red Pineau.
