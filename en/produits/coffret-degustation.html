Tasting set

Three organic Cognacs in 20 cl bottles: Conviction VSOP, Cohesion Napoléon and Transmission XO.

This set brings together three Cognacs from the Esprit Organic range, each in a 20 cl bottle: Conviction VSOP, Cohesion Napoléon and Transmission XO. All three are organically grown, from the Fins Bois cru, at Domaine de la Grande Versenne. A simple way to compare three ages of Cognac, or to give them as a gift.

In the set

  • Conviction VSOP — 20 cl: A rounded and generous organic Cognac, expressing candied fruit, warm oak and spice.
  • Cohesion Napoléon — 20 cl: A balanced organic Cognac with length, peppery notes, dried fruit and a fresh finish.
  • Transmission XO — 20 cl: A structured and generous organic Cognac with black cherry, dried flowers and rancio notes.
